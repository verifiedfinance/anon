\caption{\textbf{Answer-independent symbolic verification.} The proof
obligation is assembled from the filing alone: operand values are
\emph{grounded} in the evidence and the identity is taken from the 10-K's XBRL
calculation linkbase, not from the model's answer. The claim is asserted as a
tolerance constraint that must hold jointly with the grounded facts:
\texttt{unsat} means no assignment satisfies them together, so the claim is
inconsistent with the source (computed $62{,}151$ vs.\ claimed $65{,}000$) and
the answer is \textsc{violated}. A correct answer yields \texttt{sat} — the
claim is satisfiable against the evidence — and is \textsc{verified}.}
