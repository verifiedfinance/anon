\caption{\textbf{Verification grounded in the XBRL calculation linkbase (Nvidia
FY2026).} Gross profit is a reported subtotal, so its formula, operand tags, and
weights are read directly from the filing's calculation linkbase, which declares
$\texttt{us-gaap:GrossProfit} = (+1)\,\texttt{us-gaap:Revenues} +
(-1)\,\texttt{us-gaap:CostOfRevenue}$. In the redacted setting the model must
compute the subtotal; it returns $155{,}000$, though the filing gives $153{,}463$.
Deployed directly this ships as correct. VerifiQA (right) grounds the check
end-to-end in the filing: it takes the identity and the operand concepts from the
linkbase and binds each to its authoritative companyfacts value for FY2026
($215{,}938$, $62{,}475$), then discharges the SMT proof obligation. The
reconstruction is $153{,}463$, the claim is $1{,}537$ outside tolerance $\tau$,
Z3 returns \texttt{unsat}, and $153{,}463$ is delivered as the certified
correction. Because the formula and operands come from the filing's own
structure, a look-alike tag or an altered formula cannot be substituted.}
