\caption{\textbf{The VerifiQA pipeline.} From a filing and a question, two
independent lanes are built. \textbf{Lane A} lets the language model read the
evidence and emit a claim $c$; this lane is untrusted. \textbf{Lane B}
reconstructs the check from the filing itself: it classifies the metric,
locates the (redacted) subtotal in the filing's XBRL calculation linkbase, takes
the formula and operand \emph{concepts} from that structure, and binds each
operand to its authoritative XBRL value. The two lanes meet only at the SMT
\emph{proof obligation}, which asserts the operands, the filing's identity, and
the claim within tolerance $\tau$, and is discharged by Z3 in a single pass:
\textsc{sat}$\Rightarrow$\textsc{verified}, \textsc{unsat}$\Rightarrow$\textsc{violated}
(returning the certified correction $c^{*}$), error$\Rightarrow$\textsc{abstain}.
Two optional, bounded repair loops (dashed) each re-verify their result, so
soundness is preserved: on \textsc{violated} the model is re-asked once with
diagnostic feedback that never reveals $c^{*}$; on an unparseable SMT the encoding
is regenerated once with the solver's parse error. The verdict never depends on
trusting the model, because the check is derived entirely from the filing.}
\label{fig:pipeline}
